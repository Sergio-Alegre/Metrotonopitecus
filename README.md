# METRONOTONOPITECUS

Wellcome to my first project for CardputerADV. Is also a development of the software that will allow me later on to build my own metronomes based on ESP32 S3. So, in theory, it will be just "copy and paste" and solder the components separated. Is already going to be more complicated than that because for sure I will update the microphone to get a better accuracy on the lower and higher part of the register. 

---

## The Metronome

It includes a tap beat mode, from 20 to 300bpm (I would say that more than enough) and some subdivisions. For the pitch of the beep sound, I was starting to get too creative and after sharing it with some friends, I went back to just one pitch but different volumes. Is probably less disturbing.

For now the metronome I would say that it will stay like this, let's see after a couple of months of use. I find the LED and the volume way more clear and loud than my digital metronomes, so I hope it helps, specially for chamber music rehearsals. We all have been in the uncomfortable rehearsal playing piano to be able to hear the beep.

## A game inside a metronome?

Yes, why not? is a non brainer way of disconnect from practice. I remember my bachelor's days, before instagram era, with my 2nd generation iPod touch, the amount of brakes that I just played 5 min of angry birds, small siesta on the couch and then keep going with the practice. 

Even nowdays, I find myself that the best break from practice, photo editing or computer projects is some quick Mario Karts. Well, even better is just doing absolutly nothing, but we need to amouse ourselves from time to time. So better a simple game on the metronome, than touching the diabolical gadget with our entire lives that we call smartphone. 

Have fun!

## Special thanks

Many thanks to the folks of https://tonemap.live to share their code on github https://github.com/crossrhythm/tonemap.live that allowed me to incorporate their incredible function on my design. You will find it by pressing twice the Tunner key (T).

Is the same logic regarding, but translated to a different programming language, since the original code is written in html. For now all the basic options + transposing notes + a second palette of colours (a bit more radical and non made for daltonics) are included.

Next on the roadmap is to adopt also the different temperaments, that could be very usefull for barroque and curious people.
